Privacy policy.

How count501 collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or use our application.

Introduction

At count501, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our darts scoring application. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not access the website or application.

Information we collect

Personal data

This may include your name, email address and telephone number when you voluntarily provide it through registration, contact or related activities. You are not obliged to provide personal information, but some features may not be available without it.

Technical data

Our systems may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser, operating system, access times and pages viewed before or after accessing the website or application.

Game data

We may collect information related to darts performance, scores, statistics and playing patterns when you use the scoring application.

How we use information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Generate game statistics and performance analytics.
  • Compile anonymous statistical data for internal analysis.
  • Enable communications and respond to requests.
  • Improve the efficiency, security and operation of the service.
  • Monitor usage and trends and notify you of relevant updates.

Disclosure of information

Legal requirements and protection of rights

We may disclose information when necessary to respond to legal process, investigate possible policy violations or protect the rights, property and safety of others, as permitted by law.

Service providers

We may share data with service providers working on our behalf, such as hosting, analytics, email delivery and customer service providers, where necessary to operate the service.

Interactions with other users

When you interact with other users, they may see information associated with your profile and activity where the application makes this clear.

Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No security measure or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee protection against every form of interception or misuse.

Children

We do not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 13. If you become aware that we have collected such data, please contact us.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct or delete account information by using the available account settings or contacting us. Some information may be retained where necessary to prevent fraud, troubleshoot problems, enforce terms or meet legal obligations.

Contact us

For questions about this privacy policy, contact count501 at count501legal@gmail.com or use our contact form.
